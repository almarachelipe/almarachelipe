About

Alma Rachel Ipe

I am a fourth year Textile and Apparel Design student at the National Institute of Design, Andhra Pradesh. What draws me most to textiles is the patience and process behind it all.


My practice sits at the intersection of material research, craft, and design, with a particular interest in understanding what fibres can become beyond their conventional applications. Weaving and surface development are where I lose track of time.


I come from Wayanad, so wherever I am, I tend to look for the nearest hill. Flowers, long walks and unfamiliar places find their way into my work more often than I plan for.


I want to be a designer who creates with intention, where every material choice, every structure and every detail has a reason behind it.
