Kaldi

Advanced Print Design

A home textile print collection drawn from the wooden cutlery of Udayagiri, Nellore. The carved spoons and forks of this centuries old craft became the motif for a full range for the home: wallpaper, table linen, cushions, curtains and a carpet, each planned for screen printing.

The Collection

The collection carries the cutlery across the home. Teal and deep blue anchor it, with brown, red, burgundy and cream around them.

Colourways

The collection in four colour directions. Each board sets the wallpaper, table linen, cushions and curtains together above the palette that built it.
