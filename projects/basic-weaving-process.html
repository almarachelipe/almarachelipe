Scarlet & Sage

From a bird to a striped warp

How the colours of the plum headed parakeet became a yarn palette, a stripe rhythm, and the final woven sample.

01

Colour from the Bird

The final sample started with the plum headed parakeet, one of the birds of Andhra Pradesh. Its plum head, green body and blue tail gave the palette.

02

Yarn Dictionary

Before weaving, I collected yarns in those colours and catalogued them, noting the fibre and count of each one.

03

Weft Explorations

The yarns were tested as weft, first in polyester and then in unconventional yarns, to see how colour and texture behaved once woven.

04

Proportion and Rhythm

Small paintings of the bird’s colours were turned into stripes, then cut into strips and rearranged to try different proportions and rhythms.

05

Final Weft Colour Proportion

Three stripe plans were chosen for the weft. Each painted plan is shown with the sample woven from it.

06

Final Warp Calculation

The warp was planned as three 4 inch sections on a 38 reed, with 130, 150 and 149 ends, plus 12 selvedge ends on each side.
