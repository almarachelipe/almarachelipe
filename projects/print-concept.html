Print

Tendrils

Surface Design  ·  Print Concept  ·  2024–2025

One climbing plant, Poranopsis paniculata, carried across two courses. In Surface Design its tendrils, small white flowers and heart shaped leaves were drawn into a black and white jaal. Print Concept continued from there, taking the same jaal through two briefs: one answering the painting Weeping Grace by Jayasri Burman, and one answering the design language of the brand The Shop.

Part One. Surface Design: the jaal in black and white

The first collection. The plant was drawn as a jaal, a buta and a bel, set to size, placed in a layout, and then rendered with a stippled ground that pushes the motif forward in white.

Part Two. Print Concept: the jaal after Weeping Grace

The same jaal developed again in answer to the painting Weeping Grace by Jayasri Burman. The final version carries the density and jewel tones of the painting into a continuous repeat, with colour and emphasis spread evenly across the unit.

Part Three. Print Concept: collection for The Shop

The jaal, with its buta and bel, rendered in the brand's language of fine outlines and pastel grounds, and placed on products for the table.
