Ikat

Warp ikat stole  ·  2026

One tied and dyed warp, woven as a single stole that moves through six structures. The motif stays the same from end to end; the weave and the weft keep changing how it reads. Woven in 2/60s cotton, with the warp dyed in vat dyes.

01

The Trend

The project began with Constructive Chaos, a WGSN trend direction about the right to play: clashing brights, bold geometric pattern and a maximalist approach to colour.

02

The Reference: Yinka Ilori

Looking for an artist who works in that spirit led me to Yinka Ilori, a British-Nigerian artist and designer based in London. His work draws on his Nigerian heritage and brings loud, joyful colour and pattern to furniture, public spaces and installations. What I took from him was the way a few simple rounded shapes, repeated and stacked, can carry a whole surface.

03

Artwork

From that study I drew two motifs of my own, one of leaf shapes crossed by circles and one of tall hourglass columns, and redrew them square by square as graphs that could be tied into a warp.
